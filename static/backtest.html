How to Use Backtest Lab
This page tests your saved tickers against a repeatable swing-trading rule set. Use it as a decision-support worksheet: build a watchlist on the screener, run the weekly preset here, then monitor the Opportunity Queue, Recent Rebalances, and Exit Pressure during the week. The results are historical and cached-data based, so treat them as evidence to review rather than a trade instruction.
