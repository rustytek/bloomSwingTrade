Per-Strategy Scorecard what you actually got vs what the backtest said to expect
Drift is your realized average R minus the tested expected R. A negative drift is only meaningful once there are enough closed trades to judge — below that threshold the verdict stays "monitoring", which means too few trades to judge, not "broken". Where there is no tested benchmark, the drift column says so rather than implying underperformance.
