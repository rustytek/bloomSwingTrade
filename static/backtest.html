Strategy Lab
This is the evidence bench. The edge matrix below asks one question of every strategy: does it actually make money in the market regimes it claims to be built for? Underneath, the walk-forward tester runs a single strategy in detail — either as a rotation (ranking only) or as the trade plan you would really trade, with stops, targets and position sizing simulated. Everything here is historical and cached-data based. Read the Caveats panel before you believe a number.

Edge Matrix strategy × market regime — does the evidence back the tag?
Rows are strategies, columns are the four market regimes. Each cell shows the average return per tested rebalance period in that regime, the win rate with its Wilson confidence interval, and n — how many periods that is based on. A cell marked mis-tagged means the strategy is declared for that regime but the data says it loses money there. Cells with too thin a sample are struck through and hatched: they are not evidence.
